Customer ID

Customer ID is a 2.5D hybrid cashier simulator with pixel and 3D visuals. Set in a semi-chaotic Filipino supermarket, you play as a cashier managing queues, scanning items, checking IDs, and making difficult calls when things go sideways. The game fuses financial realism and satire in quick-paced mechanics that test both your ethics and speed.

Lead Developer Lead Designer Artist Sound Director Sound Artist UI/UX

Foam & Fortune

Foam & Fortune is a roguelike, isometric bubble-themed action game where players take control of Bubbleman, a sentient bubble born from dish soap and divine spark. Tasked with cleansing the corrupted underworld of a sink, players will fight through waves of dirt, pests, and decay.
